2. Lokal gespeicherte Projekt- und Einstellungsdaten

Tinkerbox ist eine Programmierumgebung. Die App speichert Projekte, Programmcode, Projektdateien, Sprite- und Animationsdaten, Projektversionen sowie Einstellungen im app-internen Speicher des Geräts, damit diese zwischen App-Starts verfügbar bleiben.

Frei eingegebene Projektinhalte können personenbezogene Daten enthalten. Die App übermittelt diese Inhalte nicht an einen Server von Philipp Hempel oder an Dritte.

Beim Löschen der App werden die im App-Speicher abgelegten Daten in der Regel vom Gerät entfernt. Dateien, die Sie außerhalb der App speichern oder exportieren, müssen Sie gegebenenfalls selbst löschen.

3. Import von Bildern

Wenn Sie ein Bild für ein Sprite auswählen, greift Tinkerbox ausschließlich auf die von Ihnen ausgewählte Datei zu. Das Bild wird lokal verarbeitet und nicht an Philipp Hempel oder an Dritte übermittelt. Die App verlangt keinen allgemeinen Zugriff auf den externen Gerätespeicher.

Tinkerbox sendet diese Inhalte nicht selbst an einen KI-Dienst und enthält keine integrierte Online-KI-Funktion. Prüfen Sie daher vor dem Einfügen, ob die Inhalte vertrauliche oder personenbezogene Daten enthalten.

Die Einstellung und Verarbeitung solcher Backups richten sich nach den Datenschutzinformationen Ihres Geräte- bzw. Kontenanbieters.

2. Project and settings data stored locally

Tinkerbox is a programming environment. The app stores projects, source code, project files, sprite and animation data, project versions and settings in the app-private storage on your device so that they remain available between app launches.

Freely entered project content may contain personal data. The app does not transmit this content to a server operated by Philipp Hempel or to third parties.

When the app is deleted, data stored in the app’s storage is generally removed from the device. Files that you save or export outside the app may need to be deleted separately by you.

3. Importing images

When you select an image for a sprite, Tinkerbox accesses only the file you selected. The image is processed locally and is not transmitted to Philipp Hempel or to third parties. The app does not request broad access to external device storage.

4. Clipboard and external AI services

At your request, Tinkerbox can copy task packages, feedback or image descriptions to the clipboard. A task package may contain your requested change, contents of Tinkerbox project files, information about sprite and animation files, and information from earlier external AI reviews.

Tinkerbox does not send this content to an AI service itself and contains no integrated online AI feature. If you paste and submit copied content to an external AI service, that service’s privacy policy applies. Please check whether the content contains confidential or personal information before pasting it.

5. Android system backup

The app does not explicitly disable Android's system backup and restore feature. Depending on your device, Android version and account settings, locally stored app data may therefore be included in a system-managed backup. Such backups are governed by the privacy information of your device or account provider.

6. No advertising, analytics, accounts or cloud synchronisation

According to the reviewed app code, Tinkerbox uses no advertising, analytics, crash reporting, user accounts, or cloud synchronisation. The published app does not transmit project or usage content to a server operated by Philipp Hempel.

7. Support by email

If you contact us by email, we process the information in your message, including your email address, subject and message, to handle your request.

The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is communicating with you and handling support and feedback requests. The address hephisapps@gmail.com is processed through Gmail, so Google may receive the data as the email service provider. Support emails are deleted six months after the request has been resolved, unless a statutory retention obligation applies.

GitHub may process technical usage data when the site is accessed, including IP address, browser and device information, time of access, referrer and pages viewed, in order to provide and secure the website. Further information is available in GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

The reviewed privacy-policy page contains no separate analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts.
